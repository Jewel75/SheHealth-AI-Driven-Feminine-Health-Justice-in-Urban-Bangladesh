
What need or challenge is your solution addressing in your local context? 

Bangladesh faces significant challenges in women's health, particularly concerning non-communicable diseases like cancer. According to The Guardian (April 13, 2023), there are approximately 17,686 new cases and 10,362 deaths from cervical cancer among Bangladeshi women each year. With over 50 million women at risk, widespread screening and prevention efforts are crucial. A 2025 study by BSMMU reveals that breast cancer accounts for 36.4% of female cancers, followed by cervical (11.1%), lip/oral cavity (10.1%), thyroid (7.1%), and ovarian (5.1%) cancers. Limited access to early detection and specialized treatment, combined with socio-economic barriers, cultural stigmas, and lack of awareness, restricts healthcare engagement. Menstrual hygiene management and reproductive health services are particularly insufficient for marginalized women. These challenges underscore the urgent need for technology-enabled, inclusive healthcare solutions tailored to women's unique needs.

Describe the solution you are proposing and how it is solving the challenges you described in the previous question. 

Inogen System propose to enhance the existing private healthcare system through the implementation of an AI-driven platform designed to provide accessible, affordable, and confidential feminine healthcare—addressing urgent challenges like breast and cervical cancer, menstrual hygiene, and reproductive health. Specifically tailored to marginalized women in urban Bangladesh.  The platform will overcome cultural, social, and economic barriers through inclusive digital tools, multilingual support, and community engagement strategies. This initiative will reduce disparities in health outcomes, promote early detection, and empower women to actively participate in their healthcare journey.
Intervention package: 
•	An AI-powered voice bot, compatible with basic mobile phones, will be utilized for household and community-based screening and awareness related to women's cancers, menstrual hygiene, and reproductive health. This approach aims to overcome literacy and cultural barriers effectively.
•	A coordinated mobile-based referral mechanism connecting primary to tertiary care facilities for efficient case management.
•	Electronic medical record systems (EMRs) deployed at primary health care center (e.g. Alo Clinic, Smiling Sun Clinics) for improved patient tracking and data-driven decision-making.
•	AI algorithms for personalized risk prediction, treatment guidance, and real-time monitoring through wearable tech and mobile apps.
•	Telemedicine integration at the primary care level, expanding specialist access at the community level.
•	Supply chain and inventory management systems to ensure availability of essential feminine health commodities and heygine products at low cost.
•	Mobile app listing all financial schemes for women, both governmental and non-governmental 
•	Build partnerships among private healthcare providers, local suppliers and primary health care centers to ensure service affordability and sustainability. 

How are you using the technology(ies)? 

The project uses frontier technologies to bridge healthcare access gaps:
•	IVR (Interactive Voice Response) system enabling an AI-powered voice bot to deliver reproductive health and cancer screening information in local languages.
•	Basic mobile phone compatibility ensures inclusion across socio-economic groups.
•	Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) for improved patient profiling and referrals.
•	AI-based analytics for risk prediction, diagnostic support, and patient follow-up.
•	Telemedicine platforms allow specialist consultation from remote clinics.
•	Digital inventory systems enhance commodity availability and reduce stockouts.
•	Digital patient feedback mechanism and doctor’s appointment system at targeted referral hospitals.
This blend of voice chat bot, mobile, AI, and cloud technology creates an ecosystem that offers timely information, continuity of care, and personalized support while maintaining data privacy and cultural relevance.

What are your project targets/milestones for the next 12 months? 

In the first three months:
•	Develop partnerships with healthcare facilities and obtain the required government approvals.
•	Hire a technology provider to develop and test the voice bot prototype with input from local communities, electronic medical record system, digitalize patient feedback mechanism, and doctor’s appointment system.
•	Develop training modules and organize training sessions for existing health providers at primary healthcare centers.

Between the fourth and tenth month:
•	Implement household and community-based cancer screening systems and AI-powered early cancer detection systems.
•	Establish a patient referral system from primary to tertiary healthcare centers, including a patient feedback mechanism and doctor’s appointment system with targeted referral healthcare centers.
•	Establish telemedicine services at primary healthcare centers.

In the last two months:
•	Document the business model, including the breakeven strategy
•	Disseminate the model to the government and private sector for scale-up.


Provide an overview of the capital and other contributions to the project (capital, human resources, assets, other investments and loans) 

The project will be funded through a combination of grants and contributions from local private sector. A multidisciplinary team, including public health professionals, M&E specialists, and community outreach workers, will contribute their expertise at minimal cost. Existing mobile network infrastructure will be used to deploy the voice bot, along with mobile apps for the referral mechanism, minimizing the need for additional hardware. Local partners will provide in-kind contributions, such as primary healthcare centers for training sessions and promotional activities. Partnerships with private hospitals will facilitate telemedicine services using their existing setups. Continuous monitoring and evaluation by the existing human resources of primary healthcare centers will ensure the project's smooth operation. Improved quality and ethical practices by private healthcare centers and hospitals will enhance client satisfaction, increasing patient influx and resulting in financial benefits that contribute to the model's sustainability. This resource-efficient model leverages existing assets and human capital for scalability.
